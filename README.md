# Module 01: Introduction to AI & Python

## 1. Basics of AI & Machine Learning
- **Artificial Intelligence (AI):** Building computer systems capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence (such as reasoning, problem-solving, and understanding language).
- **Machine Learning (ML):** A subset of AI where algorithms learn patterns from historical data to make predictions or decisions without being explicitly programmed for every rule.

## 2. Difference Between AI, ML, and Deep Learning
- **AI (Broadest):** Any technique that enables machines to mimic human intelligence.
- **ML (Subset of AI):** Algorithms that improve automatically through experience and data.
- **Deep Learning (Subset of ML):** Uses multi-layered artificial neural networks to learn complex patterns from large datasets (such as images, audio, and text).

## 3. Real-World AI Applications
- **Recommendation Systems:** Suggesting movies or products on platforms like Netflix and Amazon.
- **Voice Assistants:** Speech recognition and natural language understanding in Siri and Google Assistant.
- **Healthcare Diagnostics:** Detecting anomalies in X-rays and MRI scans.
- **Spam & Fraud Detection:** Flagging suspicious bank transactions and spam emails.
